Shortest Route Finder

An interactive map of 85 real neighborhoods across the Kathmandu Valley, connected by 150 roads, that finds the shortest route between any two places using Dijkstra's algorithm. Click a node for your start, click another for your destination — or use the dropdowns — then find the shortest route. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan.
